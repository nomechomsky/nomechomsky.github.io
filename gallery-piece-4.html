Untitled
2025 Study

A close study of weight, contour, and the material qualities of fired clay. The work is deliberately reduced to its essentials, so the structure and surface remain central.

Its quiet palette and sculptural shape make it a useful counterpoint to the more overtly expressive works in the collection.
