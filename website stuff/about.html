About

Working from my studio in Spike Island in Bristol- I have been working as a self taught ceramic artist for around a year, working on process, intuition and the joy of making things. I have been recently infatuated with pottery, functional and decorative stuff. Vases, cups, mugs, bowls. I mix glazes to create different colours and textures- decorating based on inspiration from research and often mark making based off of intuition. I love ceramics however I feel a change in direction coming round the corner. I also have my own art practice outside of cermaics- see my instagram 'Nomechomsky'.
